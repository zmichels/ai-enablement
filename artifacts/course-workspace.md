# Course workspace

Create only the parts needed now. These are templates, not evidence that files or course objects exist. Use real document paths or URLs in the finished index; write “not created” or “access not checked” instead of inventing links.

## Course index

Course identity, audience, delivery mode, instructor, term/version, LMS/course URL and draft/published state. Record the authoritative source when multiple versions exist.

If rewriting an extracted objective, retain its original wording and source locator alongside the proposal. If constructing a syllabus from an approved objective list, cite that list and use the same IDs. Course-wide objectives and module objectives may have a parent/child relationship; do not silently merge them.

## Syllabus outline

Course purpose and audience; prerequisites; approved objectives; module schedule and milestones; learning activities and workload; assessment and grading approach; materials and source links; accessibility and support; relevant institution-supplied policies. Mark missing dates, policy wording and credit requirements as unresolved rather than inventing them.

## Evaluation and revision record

Record the external evaluator/report, date, scope, course version, method, response count/rate when available and evidence limitations.

| Finding and source locator | Affected objective / artifact | Interpretation and uncertainty | Proposed change | Decision / owner | Later check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awaiting report | To map | Not assessed | None yet | Pending | To define |

An external quality review, learner survey and learning assessment answer different questions. Preserve contradictory findings. State what is accepted, deferred or rejected and why; update affected artifacts only within the instructor's authorization. Preserve the previous version and links to the change rationale.

## LMS handoff

Map each document to its intended course/module/page/assignment location. Record what was inspected, edited and verified, including permissions and learner visibility. Link to the actual course object after it exists. A local draft or upload plan is not a live course.
