# Build and improve a course

Use [the course-building role](../roles/course-builder.md) for ongoing help and [the course workspace](../artifacts/course-workspace.md) for a linked plan. Start with existing documents or one useful next artifact, not an empty stack of forms.

## Start from either direction

With a syllabus: identify its version, extract objectives with page or section references, and map them to modules and assessments. Keep extracted wording distinct from a suggested rewrite.

With objectives: confirm the audience and intended performance, design evidence that would demonstrate it, then build activities and the syllabus around the same objective IDs. Link the original objective list. When sources disagree, ask which governs rather than silently choosing.

Generate a document when it fills a real gap. Use available document/file tools and the instructor's preferred format; otherwise supply an editable draft. Keep a course index with real links, source/version, status and dependencies. Do not claim that a file has been saved or a link is accessible without checking.

## Understand the LMS before changing it

Learning management systems commonly organize content, activities, assessment, learner access and reporting. Similar names can hide different behavior. Confirm the institution, tenant URL, course shell, term, role and interface before translating a plan into the LMS.

| Platform | Useful orientation | What to verify in the actual course |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | Modules, pages, assignments, rubrics and course navigation | Course template, item/course publishing, dates and student-visible sequence |
| D2L Brightspace | Content organization, activities, assessment and release/completion conditions | Classic versus newer content experience, local template and availability settings |
| Blackboard Learn | Course Content and learning modules; Original and Ultra views differ | Actual view, containers, visibility and institutional tool choices |
| Moodle | Course sections, resources, activities and completion tracking | Version, enabled plugins, completion settings and role permissions |

These are orientation cues, not tested click paths or installed integrations. A requested platform-specific operation should consult current official and institutional guidance. Do not assume SCORM, LTI, QTI or course-package imports are enabled or interchangeable; confirm the required format/version before preparing an import.

## Public institutional references

Checked October 8, 2026. These establish starting points, not access to an instructor's course.

- [University of Arizona: Brightspace Next](https://ucatt.arizona.edu/technologies/brightspace-next) identifies D2L Brightspace as its LMS. Do not default an Arizona course to Blackboard from an older recollection.
- [Arizona instructional design services](https://ucatt.arizona.edu/course-design/instructional-design-services) describes support for online course development and evaluation. Use its local support route when relevant.
- [University of Minnesota: set up your Canvas course site](https://teachingsupport.umn.edu/prepare-teach/set-your-canvas-course-site) identifies Canvas and points instructors toward applicable campus templates.
- [Blackboard: create learning modules](https://help.anthology.com/blackboard/instructor/en/course-and-content-management/create-containers-for-content/create-learning-modules.html) and [Original quick start](https://help.anthology.com/blackboard/instructor/en/original-course-view/getting-started/quick-start.html) help distinguish content organization and course views.
- [Moodle activity completion](https://docs.moodle.org/404/en/Activity_completion) is a versioned reference. Check the installed version and whether completion tracking is enabled before using it.

Local templates, permissions and required policies take precedence over generic platform advice when they apply. An affiliation or partnership does not establish shared accounts, licensing, templates or access.

## Browser and computer use

Use a connector or authorized export when it provides the needed evidence. If the portal requires a browser or desktop interface, use the host's available browser/computer-use tools in the instructor's authorized session. Read the current screen before acting; do not guess menus or report unobserved content.

Inspect the course identity, structure, file links, dates, release rules and learner preview. Keep private observations in the approved workspace. If sign-in, MFA or a missing tool blocks access, explain the specific gap and ask for the user step or an approved export; continue planning with available evidence.

For authorized edits, identify the intended course and draft state, preserve a recoverable previous version, apply a bounded change and verify the result. Consider whether autosave, publishing, announcements or due-date changes affect learners immediately. Planning does not itself authorize publication, enrollment, grading, messages or permission changes. Follow the host's confirmation rules and the user's actual authorization.

## Learn from course evaluations

A third-party evaluation can reveal unclear instructions, weak alignment or navigation problems. Preserve its author/source, date, course version, method, sample and limits. Link findings to specific objectives and artifacts. Separate the evaluator's conclusion from your interpretation and the instructor's decision.

Compare like measures: satisfaction is not demonstrated learning; completion is not mastery. Missing response counts or changed assessments limit comparison. Do not infer causation from a score change alone. Propose a concrete revision, its expected effect and a later check; record accepted, deferred and rejected recommendations. Keep intended outcomes and observed results in separate records.

## Small trial

Use synthetic material: two supplied objectives, one unassessed objective, a syllabus with an outdated due date, and an external review saying navigation was confusing without giving a response count. A useful assistant preserves objective sources, flags the alignment/date gaps, drafts a linked revision plan and labels the review's limits. It does not fabricate learner results, document links or a successful LMS update.

This module is authored guidance. Structural and prompt-assembly checks do not establish instructional effectiveness or live LMS capability.
