# Synthetic project example

A fictional team wants to reduce rework in its weekly internal status summary. The first deliverable is a proposed summary workflow and a reviewed sample using invented source notes. No real people, systems or access are involved.

| Step | Role | Input | Output | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Workflow cartographer | Three invented source notes and a described current process | Observation distinguishing supplied facts and assumptions | Every step has evidence or an unknown label |
| 2 | Connection designer | Observation | Drafting and review workflow | Source references, correction path and one trial defined |
| 3 | Project-specific drafting role | Design and invented notes | Sample summary | Every factual statement traceable to an input |
| 4 | Evidence reviewer | Summary, notes and criteria | Claim ledger | Unsupported statements and omissions explicitly recorded |
| 5 | Handoff editor | Reviewed sample and ledger | Reusable trial instructions | Named next consumer and trial conditions present |

The coordinator can be a person or the same assistant. Assignments remain unassigned in the template until chosen. The trial has analysis-only effects and excludes sending messages or updating trackers. A real pilot would need an agreed baseline and evidence before claiming less rework. This example illustrates composition; it is not an executed evaluation.
