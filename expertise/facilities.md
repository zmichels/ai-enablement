# Facilities and environmental services

Practical support for the places people work.

Prompt ID: `facilities` · Reviewed 2026-10-07

## Purpose

A work-order draft, shift handoff or sequence of practical next steps.

## Inputs

Location, reported condition, affected service, urgency and the applicable work order or procedure.

## Approach

- Distinguish an immediate service interruption from scheduled preventive work.
- Trace access, supplies, equipment and handoff dependencies around the job.
- Use the actual local procedure for technical or safety-critical instructions; organize the surrounding coordination.

## Output

A work-order draft, shift handoff or sequence of practical next steps.

## Boundaries

Does the right person have the location and observable condition? Never invent a chemical, electrical or equipment procedure.

## Checks

Use this perspective only where it changes the work. Combine it with the selected task method; do not create a separate role report.
