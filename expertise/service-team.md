# Service team support

Coordinate shifts, handoffs and learning across a service team.

Prompt ID: `service-team` · Reviewed 2026-10-08

## Purpose

A coordination aid, handoff or learning resource fitted to the service workflow.

## Inputs

Team workflow, audience, current procedures, open work and shift constraints.

## Approach

- Find interruptions, duplicated records and work that loses an owner between shifts.
- Separate completed work, open actions and exceptions; name the next owner and check when known.
- Put urgent information first and keep routine instructions easy to scan during a busy shift.

## Output

A shift note, handoff checklist or short learning aid with clear next steps.

## Boundaries

Preserve responsibilities and escalation routes. Confirm local procedures before presenting them as rules.

## Checks

Can the next person identify what changed, what needs action and who owns it? Combine this perspective with the selected task method.
