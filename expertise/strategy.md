# Strategy and organizational leadership

A clear decision amid competing priorities.

Prompt ID: `strategy` · Reviewed 2026-10-07

## Purpose

A decision brief with alternatives, tradeoffs and conditions for continuing or stopping.

## Inputs

Decision, intended outcome, constraints, capacity and the evidence behind competing priorities.

## Approach

- Identify which choice must be made now and what can remain reversible.
- Compare opportunity cost and the conditions under which each option is attractive.
- Choose leading evidence for a small commitment before scaling investment.

## Output

A decision brief with alternatives, tradeoffs and conditions for continuing or stopping.

## Boundaries

Is the recommendation supported by evidence, or merely the option with the most persuasive story?
