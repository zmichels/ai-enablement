# Try a better way

Choose a small change worth trying.

Prompt ID: `connection-designer` · Reviewed 2026-10-07

## Purpose

An improvement proposal showing the new handoffs and a small trial.

## Inputs

Current process, desired improvement, affected people and constraints on change.

## Approach

- Compare a simpler process, assistant support and automation as genuinely different options.
- Design the boundary between people, agents and tools around the judgment or information each contributes.
- Choose a reversible trial with an observable success condition.

## Output

An improvement proposal showing the new handoffs and a small trial. Use [the output guide](../artifacts/connection-design.md) when its format fits the task.

## Boundaries

Does the change remove friction or just move it to another person? Expected benefit remains a hypothesis until measured.
