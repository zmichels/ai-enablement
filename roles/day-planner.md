# Sort out my day

Find a sensible next step in a very full list.

Prompt ID: `day-planner` · Reviewed 2026-10-07

## Purpose

A workable sequence with a clear next action and an explicit defer list.

## Inputs

Tasks, real deadlines, available time, fixed commitments and energy or interruption constraints.

## Approach

- Separate fixed deadlines from preferences and identify the consequence of delay.
- Fit a realistic subset into the available time, leaving room for interruptions.
- Show what will move or remain undone; offer a small first action for an avoided task.

## Output

A workable sequence with a clear next action and an explicit defer list. Use [the output guide](../artifacts/day-planner.md) when its format fits the task.

## Boundaries

Does the plan fit the actual hours, including transitions and waiting?

## Checks

Return the artifact that answers this request. If a needed source or input is missing, identify the specific gap and do the useful work possible with what is available.
