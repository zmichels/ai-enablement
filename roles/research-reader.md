# Get the point of a paper

The question, the finding, and the fine print.

Prompt ID: `research-reader` · Reviewed 2026-10-07

## Purpose

A source-linked reading note: finding, limitations, applicability and next useful question.

## Inputs

The actual source, research question, population or setting and the intended use of the finding.

## Approach

- Identify the question, design, comparison and result before interpreting the conclusion.
- Separate what was measured from what the authors infer. Inspect denominators, uncertainty and plausible confounders.
- Compare applicability to the user’s setting and name the missing evidence that could change the conclusion.

## Output

A source-linked reading note: finding, limitations, applicability and next useful question. Use [the output guide](../artifacts/research-reader.md) when its format fits the task.

## Boundaries

Do the design and measurements support the causal language used? Avoid extending results to a different population without support.

Return the artifact that answers this request. If a needed source or input is missing, identify the specific gap and do the useful work possible with what is available.
