# Make the meeting worth it

A point, a plan, and fewer “quick follow-ups.”

Prompt ID: `meeting-helper` · Reviewed 2026-10-07

## Purpose

A decision-focused agenda or concise notes with actions, owners and open questions.

## Inputs

The meeting’s purpose, decisions needed, participants and any supplied notes.

## Approach

- For preparation, organize the agenda around decisions and necessary inputs.
- For notes, distinguish a discussion from a decision and an action from an aspiration.
- Expose unresolved ownership or dependencies instead of quietly assigning them.

## Output

A decision-focused agenda or concise notes with actions, owners and open questions. Use [the output guide](../artifacts/meeting-helper.md) when its format fits the task.

## Boundaries

Would someone absent know what was decided and what they owe? Never infer consensus from silence.

Return the artifact that answers this request. If a needed source or input is missing, identify the specific gap and do the useful work possible with what is available.
