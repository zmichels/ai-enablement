# Turn experience into reusable learning

The durable unit of learning is a small, evidence-backed change that another person or agent can inspect and use. A useful lesson says when it applies, what to do, how to check it and when not to use it.

To notice and retain useful context while work is happening, start with [Give the project a memory](guides/project-memory.md). It includes a portable skill, a paste-ready prompt, a structured ledger, and optional Decision-PGA exports for repeated observations at different scales. Capturing a candidate and demonstrating an improvement remain separate steps.

1. Capture an observation from authorized work. Preserve expected versus actual behavior and the smallest supporting evidence reference in the private project record.
2. Draft a candidate using [the lesson template](lesson-template.md). Separate observation, explanation and proposed rule. Record competing explanations and failures as well as successes.
3. Check novelty against the catalog. Amend or supersede an existing lesson when appropriate; avoid duplicate rules with different names.
4. Test the candidate against the original case, a different case and a counterexample where the rule should not apply. Use synthetic examples for broader sharing. Record correction effort and any regression; do not fabricate a comparison if no baseline exists.
5. Review evidence and applicability. Distinguish `observed`, `repeated`, `evaluated` and `recommended` maturity. A reviewer may reject or narrow the rule. Self-review must be labeled; consequential recommendations may need a separate reviewer.
6. Separately decide distribution scope. Internal observations remain internal unless an authorized process approves a generalized version. Removing names alone does not establish that a finding is safe to share.
7. With authorization, adopt the smallest useful change: a test, prompt, template, procedure, skill or conditional reference. Assign a version, owner or unassigned maintenance responsibility, review trigger and superseded reference.
8. Recheck on source change, failed use or a scheduled review owned by the maintainer. Mark stale, disputed or retired lessons explicitly. Preserve a recovery path to the previous version.

Do not let a lesson increase an agent's permissions, bypass a decision owner or claim to be institutional policy. This workflow does not retrain the model or provide cross-session memory by itself.
